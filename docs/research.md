# HTML budget tracker apps on Etsy: features, reviews and pricing

Research as of 7 November 2026 · Source doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/ASX88HHBjNwpo6ArZ7VLY4

About 60 Etsy UK listings that are HTML or web-app budget trackers, with reviews read on the 15 or so that have them. The category is very new: most listings and shops date from the last 3–6 months, so review numbers are low. The most-reviewed listing found has 44 reviews.

## What they offer

Most listings include the same core set of features:

- **"Safe to spend" today:** one number showing what you can spend, after upcoming bills, until next payday. This is now the main selling point, and it's almost always pitched as "ADHD-friendly".
- **Budgeting by paycheck:** weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly, with which bills each paycheck has to cover. Many flag months that have a third paycheck.
- **Bills:** recurring bills set up once, a bills calendar, a paid/unpaid checklist.
- **Debt payoff:** snowball or avalanche order, with a projected debt-free date.
- **Savings:** sinking funds and savings goals with progress bars.
- **Extras:** net worth, the 50/30/20 split, no-spend challenges, light/dark themes, any currency, and pasting in your bank's CSV export.
- **Backups:** export and import, because data is stored in the browser.

### How they're sold and delivered

- Nearly every seller has a **free live demo**, hosted on Vercel, GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages. Sellers lean on it hard: EzPlanningStudio makes buyers type "YES" to confirm they tried the demo before they can check out.
- The download is a PDF containing links. Sellers offer three ways to use the app: an offline HTML file; an "Add to Home Screen" version for phones; and, increasingly, a hosted version with a login that syncs between devices.
- EzPlanningStudio sends **a full activation code that unlocks the app** after purchase — the access-code approach.

## What customers like

- **Ease of use** is by far the most-praised thing. On TheLittleMachines' listing, 30 of 44 reviews mention it; on MasterMoneyBudget's, 19 of 24.
- **The safe-to-spend number.** One buyer said they'd never seen a planner work it out for them.
- **Using it on both desktop and phone.**
- **Clean, calm design**, described as "not overwhelming".
- **Responsive sellers.** Seller service comes up in a lot of reviews, often because buyers needed help getting set up.

## What customers don't like

From the 1–3 star reviews:

- **Hard to navigate.** Two 1-star reviews: "Very hard to navigate the app" and "Not really adhd friendly unfortunately".
- **Couldn't download or set it up.** One 1-star reviewer couldn't get it to download at all; another buyer struggled with setting up the calendar.
- **Saving problems.** In Safari and Firefox, each save downloads a new copy of the file instead of updating the one you have, so you end up with a pile of files.
- **Android and Samsung.** One 1-star shop review says the app is really made for Apple devices.
- **Bugs and too few features.** One 2-star review mentions glitches and non-working sections, and says the program could use more features to feel complete.
- **A risk nobody has complained about yet, but will:** data that only lives in browser storage. Clearing the browser deletes it, and there's no sync between devices. Every seller's small print warns about this.

TabulaaStudio has 8 reviews, all 5-star and all posted on 23–24 October. That pattern looks seeded, so those reviews weren't counted as evidence.

## Price point

Price paid (after sale discount) across 60 HTML / web-app budget trackers, Etsy UK search results, 7 Nov 2026:

| Price paid | Listings |
| --- | --- |
| Under £5 | 12 |
| £5–10 | 26 |
| £10–15 | 16 |
| £15–25 | 4 |
| £25+ | 2 |

- **What buyers actually pay:** the median is **£7.98**; the middle half of listings sell for £5.63–£11.99.
- **Discounts are almost permanent:** 49 of the 60 listings show a sale. The median crossed-out price is about £15.
- **The listings with real traction all sit at £10.50–£13.40:**
  - FinNestStudio: £10.51, 20 reviews, 389 favourites
  - MasterMoneyBudget: £12.21, 24 reviews, 774 favourites
  - TheLittleMachines: £13.40, 44 reviews, 3 sales in the last 24 hours
- **Higher prices can work, but only for the full package.** EzPlanningStudio sells at £25.66 with an activation code, an installable app and a strong demo. ZenfullyCo, at £50.72, has only 2 reviews.

**Recommendation:** set the list price at £16–18 and run a standing discount that brings the price paid down to about **£10–13**. Pricing under £8 puts you up against a flood of cheap clones made with AI, which buyers can't tell apart. Above £15, you need features worth paying for, such as reliable syncing or a login.

## Where to stand out

The market is crowded with near-identical "ADHD safe-to-spend" apps. The complaints point to three gaps no one has made reliable yet:

- saving that just works (on Android too, not only Apple)
- a simpler first setup
- sync between devices

A UK angle (UK pay cycles and UK household bills) is also barely covered; only TabulaaStudio, the shop with the doubtful reviews, targets the UK so far.
